Education

* Ph.D. in Astronomy & Astrophysics, Sorbonne University, Paris, France (2015)
* M.S. in Physics and Radiation Technology, University Clermont Auvergne, France (2011)

Work experience
======
* 2025 – present: Senior Postdoctoral Researcher, Catalan Institute of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (ICN2), Barcelona, Spain
  * Demonstration of microwave-magnon coupling in Yttrium Iron Garnet (YIG) micro-disks at room temperature
  * Modelling of YIG micro-disk magnetic resonance using Mumax3 software
  * Design of a superconducting circuit with ten multiplexed lumped-element LC resonators (1–2.5 GHz), with an inductor loop enabling coupling to YIG micro-disks

* 2022 – 2025: Microwave Engineer, NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory, Pasadena, CA
  * Supervision of students and junior postdoctoral scholars at Caltech, designing and characterising superconducting KIDs for the NEW-MUSIC instrument
  * Design of membrane-based superconducting lumped-element band-pass filters for a heterodyne receiver at 450–610 GHz — the first membrane-based sub-millimeter superconducting filter-bank ever demonstrated
  * Design of a 5-channel radiometer front-end at 90–182 GHz for INCUS, a NASA-funded mission to study tropical storms

* 2021 – 2022: Research Scientist, Caltech, Pasadena, CA
  * Demonstrated hydrogenated amorphous silicon (a-Si:H) with the lowest reported two-level-system (TLS) noise in amorphous dielectrics
  * Developed a backend homodyne readout system for TLS noise measurement in superconducting resonators
  * Demonstrated the first flat gradient-index silicon-only lens with 3-layer anti-reflection metamaterial structures for 155–360 GHz, achieving 99% power transmission

* 2017 – 2021: Postdoctoral Researcher, Caltech, Pasadena, CA
  * Developed a-Si:H with the lowest loss tangent reported in amorphous dielectrics (7×10⁻⁶), enabling multi-layer architectures for KIDs and superconducting qubits
  * Characterised titanium nitride KIDs; developed a fitting model for density-of-states smearing in disordered superconductors
  * Developed 2-layer metamaterial anti-reflection structures for silicon optics via DRIE, achieving >99% power transmission within 187–317 GHz

* 2012 – 2017: Ph.D. Student & Postdoctoral Researcher, Paris Observatory & French Space Agency (CNES), Paris, France
  * CIDRE instrument development — a 4-pixel heterodyne receiver at 2.7 THz to detect OH and HD molecular lines in the interstellar medium
  * Design and test of a Martin-Puplett interferometer at 2.7 THz
  * Developed a phase grating algorithm; demonstrated 4-way beam-splitting gratings at 610 GHz with state-of-the-art efficiency

* 2011 – 2012: Electronic Engineer, Academia Sinica Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics (ASIAA), Taipei, Taiwan
  * Programmed a Fourier Transform Spectrometer on an FPGA board (ROACH)
  * Built a thermally regulated IP camera to study ice formation on an ASIAA radiometer in Greenland
